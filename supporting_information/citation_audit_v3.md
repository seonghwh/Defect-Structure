## Suggested Reviewer-Safe Wording

The v3 wording is already defensible because it calls the two reduction constants "effective" and does not claim that each is a universal thermodynamic constant. The safest wording is:

> The concentration-based reduction constant K_R used here should be interpreted as an effective parameter. The undoped and Mn-bearing values retain internally consistent canonical BaTiO3 defect-model behavior while absorbing activity-coefficient, dopant-vacancy association, and carrier-trapping effects that are not represented explicitly in the simplified mass-action equations.

Avoid wording that says:

> Mn doping changes the fundamental thermodynamic standard-state K_R of the oxygen-reduction reaction.

That stronger statement would be harder to defend because the true equilibrium constant is activity-based and composition-independent at fixed standard state.
